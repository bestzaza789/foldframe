Privacy Policy

Foldframe – Responsive & Foldable Preview · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Foldframe does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It sends nothing to any server we operate.

What the extension does

When you click the Foldframe icon, it opens a preview tab showing the page you were on inside a device frame. The page loads directly from its own website into your browser, exactly as if you had opened it yourself.

Permissions

  • activeTab: reads the address of the tab you clicked the icon on, so it can open that page in the preview. Only when you click.
  • Site access (optional, per site): asked for one site at a time, only when you preview it. Used solely to allow that site to display inside the preview frame. You can remove it at any time in chrome://extensions.
  • declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess: removes the X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy response headers for frames inside the Foldframe preview tab only, on sites you have allowed. These headers normally stop a page from being shown inside another page. Your normal browsing tabs are never affected.

Data stored on your device

Your last selected device, orientation and zoom are saved in your browser's local storage so the preview reopens the same way. This never leaves your computer.

Third parties

None. Sites you preview may use their own cookies and analytics, as they would in any tab; their policies apply to them.
