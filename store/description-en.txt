See your site the way phones really show it, including the new foldables.

Click the Foldframe icon on any tab and the page opens inside a device frame at the exact CSS viewport of the device you pick.

WHAT YOU GET
• Around 20 current phones, tablets and foldables, with accurate screen sizes
• Foldables can be shown folded or unfolded, with the hinge crease visible so you can spot content that lands on the fold
• Portrait or landscape in one click
• Your own custom width and height
• Fit the device to the window, or view it at 100%
• Keyboard shortcuts: R to rotate, F to fold, Z to zoom
• Works with localhost and local dev servers
• Dark and light theme, English and Thai

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no ads, no analytics, nothing sent anywhere
• Asks for access one site at a time, only when you preview it
• Only changes headers inside its own preview tab. Your normal tabs are never touched

Screen sizes come from public device databases and are updated as new devices are released.
